Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Last Updated: October 2026

CM INVENTORY WALLET ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use CM INVENTORY WALLET, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, chosen username, agency name, and city when creating an account or requesting employee/user access.
  • Property & Inventory Data: Property plot numbers, sizing (Marla, Kanal, Sq Ft), pricing, locations, photos, and video tour links uploaded by you for inventory management.
  • Device & Usage Data: Local storage and cache data stored on your device to enable offline access and offline data synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and synchronize your real estate plot listings and inventory across your devices.
  • To authenticate and authorize user sessions and enforce administrative security.
  • To facilitate communication between agency administrators and staff regarding account activations.
  • To enable offline caching so you can access your property portfolio without an active internet connection.

3. Data Storage and Offline Operation

CM INVENTORY WALLET employs local database storage (IndexedDB / LocalStorage) on your device. This allows uninterrupted access to your real estate portfolio even when you are working in remote areas without internet coverage. When connectivity is restored, data synchronizes securely with our encrypted servers.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data or property inventory to third parties. Your listings belong to you and your authorized agency account.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) during transmission and secure password hashing algorithms to safeguard your credentials and property information.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to your personal data or request the deletion of your account and associated listings at any time. To request data deletion, contact administration via the email or phone number below.
